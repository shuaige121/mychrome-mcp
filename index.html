YOU CAN ASK

Start with what you're already doing.

These are example requests you can give your AI. Open your own page, explain the outcome, and let it help from there.

01

Update your LinkedIn page

Work in your current page using the details you provide.

“Use MyChrome to review my LinkedIn page. Update my About and experience sections with these details, then check the changes.”

02

See who's reached out on Facebook

Check your messages and notifications for what needs a response.

“Check whether anyone has contacted me on Facebook. List the messages I should reply to and tell me where you need my help.”

03

Publish your article where you choose

Facebook, Xiaohongshu, Reddit or LinkedIn—you specify the destination accounts and publishing scope.

“Publish this article I've written to the accounts I specify. Fill in what each page requires and check each published result.”

These are task ideas, not a claim that every workflow on these sites has been verified. What works depends on the website, account permissions and current page.

HAND IT OVER. TAKE IT BACK.

You and AI.
Taking turns.

AI uses your Mac's real pointer and keyboard. When a step needs you, control comes back. Complete it, then explicitly say “continue” in your AI conversation to carry on.

esc+esc

Want control back? Press Esc twice.

Ordinary input is intercepted during takeover. Double Esc stops the current agent; global pause prevents further input across agents. Stopping does not undo actions already sent.
